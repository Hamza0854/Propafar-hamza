Make your mark abroad

Advanced filtering options

Listed properties are linked to specific information which may be of interest to you as a non-local property seeker; you may require additional insight about issues that matter when navigating unfamiliar foreign real estate markets (e.g. public transport options, distance to airports… etc). You can set the filtering criteria in your mother tongue and read the property profiles in your own language. Instead of searching different local websites for each specific language and country, browse and compare foreign properties in one easy-to-use central platform, saving you considerable time and effort.

Customize currency and units of measurement

Forget the fiddly conversions: prices are displayed in the currency of your choice, independent from the location or local currency! And the property's size, distance and other details are displayed in the unit of measurement you use, whether it's metric, English units or those used elsewhere in the world.

Reach out at the best time!

Our website links real estate-related supply and demand together from all over the world. The property you are looking for may be listed by someone living in another time zone. We understand the gravity of contacting an owner at an inappropriate time of day.

An inconvenient timing of your inquiry call in another time zone (e.g. at night or at dawn) might easily destroy your chances of settling a deal. This is why as a registered user, the advertiser’s availability is displayed always converted into the local time.

Map view will help you find your way around

The map view will help you discover the particular location of a property and what the surroundings have to offer. Alternative property prices are also covered in the related area, providing you with a useful basis for comparison.

This comes in particularly handy if you are not familiar with the characteristics of a certain city, area or country. The map acts as a visual clue to help you narrow down your property listings further, so that you can choose the best value for your money.

Datasheets help shortlist your favorite items

Buying or renting a property, especially from abroad, requires careful consideration and is rarely done overnight. Thus, you can save your favorite finds for comparison and overview, sort them into categories, and easily export them into a spreadsheet to give you further filtering options. This way you can narrow your search down to the best hit!
